# Success Criteria

## Session safety

- Deployment, identity, repository, data, network, and integration boundaries are confirmed.
- All demonstrations use synthetic or approved sanitized inputs.
- Every material action is reviewed and reversible.
- No identifying, sensitive, regulated, production, or proprietary content is captured.

## Learning

- Participants can distinguish Plan, Normal, and edit-approval workflows.
- Participants can scope context to relevant files and directories.
- Participants can review a proposed plan, command, diff, and validation result.
- Participants understand that external integrations require separate approval.

## Demonstration quality

- Each scenario states an outcome, constraints, expected evidence, and fallback.
- At least one scenario shows code understanding and one shows a verified change.
- Failures are explained honestly and handled with the fallback plan.

## Follow-up

- Candidate use cases are scored by value, feasibility, risk, and evidence.
- Owners are represented by roles rather than personal names.
- Decisions, action items, and next steps are captured in sanitized form.
